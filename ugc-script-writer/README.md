# ugc-script-writer

Writes TikTok Shop scripts that sound like someone who owns the product talking to a friend.

The rules came from studying real creator videos for the patterns they shared, never their wording. The product shows up in the first few lines, benefits are concrete things you can see, and there's one soft call to action with no countdown pressure.

It never invents a backstory or a personal claim you didn't give it, and every script ends with a short list of product claims to check before you post.
